COTE Dean Administrative Teaching Equivalent (ATE) Policy: Under CTU and SUC academic governance, the Dean of the College of Technology and Engineering is granted an administrative deloading equivalent of 12.0 units for college-wide leadership overseeing BSIT, BIT, and BSIE programs. Specialized teaching assignments (up to 6.0 units in advanced, graduate, or capstone courses) complement this executive allocation without violating full-time workload ceilings.
