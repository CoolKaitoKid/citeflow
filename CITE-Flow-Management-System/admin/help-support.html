Help & Support Center

Frequently asked questions, guides, and technical support information for CITE-Flow.

Accounts

How do I add a new faculty member?

Navigate to the User Management module from the sidebar and click the "Add Faculty" button. Fill in the required details to register their account.

Workload

How are teaching units verified?

Teaching loads submitted by instructors undergo automated system validation against standard curriculum hours before landing on the approval queue.

Reports

Who can view analytics reports?

Access to comprehensive department reports and analytics exports is restricted to College Deans, Chairpersons, and authorized administrators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guides to help you resolve common administrative issues.

How do I submit files to the Document Vault?

Go to the Document Vault module, click the upload action button, select the correct document category, attach your file, and confirm the submission.

How are workflow approvals tracked?

All document reviews and status changes are automatically recorded in real-time within the Workflow Approval module and System Activity Logs.

What should I do if a system error occurs?

Ensure your connection is stable and refresh the page. If the issue persists, reach out to the technical support team using the floating chat tool.
