Faculty Resources

Help & Support Center

Frequently asked questions, guides, and assistance regarding faculty tasks and submissions.

Submissions

How do I submit tasks or documents?

Go to your Submissions page, select an assigned task, upload your required files or submission link, and click "Submit for Review".

Tracking

How can I track my review progress?

Use the Status Tracking module to monitor real-time review timelines, stage progress, and view administrative remarks or revision requests.

Reports

How do I manage MFO Accomplishment Reports?

Access your MFO Report hub directly from the dashboard or submissions view to create, update, and submit your quarterly accomplishment reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guidance on common queries within the faculty portal.

Can I update my files after submitting?

Yes, as long as the task has not reached final approval, you can replace or delete attached files before or during the review stage.

What happens if my submission is marked for revision?

Check the admin remarks on your task detail view to see what changes are needed, update your files, and click "Resubmit for Review".

Who should I contact if I encounter technical issues?

You can reach out directly to your department chairperson or the system administrator for account and workflow assistance.
